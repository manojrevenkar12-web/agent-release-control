# Export your data

Exports include tasks, comments and assignees; attachments are linked, not copied.

## Free

On the Free plan you can export each project as CSV from the project menu.

## Pro

On Pro you can export a whole workspace as CSV or JSON under Settings > Export.

## Business

On Business you can export a whole workspace as CSV or JSON and schedule a weekly export to your own storage under Settings > Export.
