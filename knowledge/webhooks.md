# Webhooks

## Free

Webhooks are available on the Business plan only.

## Pro

Webhooks are available on the Business plan only; Pro workspaces can use the Zapier integration instead.

## Business

On Business, add webhooks under Settings > Developers > Webhooks; each delivery is signed with HMAC-SHA256.
