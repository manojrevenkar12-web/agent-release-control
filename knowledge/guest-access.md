# Guests

Guests can see and comment on the projects they are invited to.

## Free

Free workspaces cannot invite guests.

## Pro

Pro workspaces can invite up to 10 guests at no cost.

## Business

Business workspaces can invite unlimited guests and restrict them to single projects.
