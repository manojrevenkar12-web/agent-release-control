# Storage limits

Storage use is shown under Settings > Billing; files in the trash still count until it is emptied.

## Free

Free workspaces have 2 GB of file storage.

## Pro

Pro workspaces have 100 GB of file storage.

## Business

Business workspaces have 1 TB of file storage.
