# Change your plan

Downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period.

## Free

To upgrade from Free, an owner opens Settings > Billing, chooses Pro (8 EUR per user per month) or Business (16 EUR per user per month) and adds a card.

## Pro

To move from Pro to Business (16 EUR per user per month), an owner opens Settings > Billing and chooses Change plan; the price difference is prorated.

## Business

You are on Business, our highest plan; for more than 500 users, contact sales for an enterprise agreement.
