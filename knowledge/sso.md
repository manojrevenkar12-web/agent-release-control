# Single sign-on (SSO)

## Free

Single sign-on is available on the Business plan. On Free, you would need to upgrade to Business to use SAML SSO.

## Pro

Single sign-on is available on the Business plan. On Pro, an admin can upgrade to Business under Settings > Billing to use SAML SSO.

## Business

On Business, an admin sets up SAML SSO under Settings > Security > SSO with Okta, Azure AD or any SAML 2.0 provider; SCIM provisioning is included.
