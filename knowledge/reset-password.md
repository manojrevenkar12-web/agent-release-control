# Reset your password

Go to the sign-in page and choose 'Forgot password'. Enter the email address on your account and we send a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder; links can only be used once. Signing in with Google or Microsoft uses that provider's password instead.
